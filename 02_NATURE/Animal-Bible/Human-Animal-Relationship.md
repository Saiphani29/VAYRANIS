# Human Animal Relationship

## Purpose

This document defines how humans and animals coexist in VAYRANIS.

The relationship is based on ecology, survival, respect, and balance.

---

# Core Principle

Animals are not only symbols or companions. They are living beings with:

- Biology
- Instinct
- Ecosystem roles
- Limitations
- Adaptations

---

# Food Chain Question

Open design question:

How does human survival interact with the ecosystem?

Future development must consider:

- Hunting
- Farming
- Domestication
- Conservation
- Natural balance

The answer should come from the world system, not only culture.
