# Prana / Natural Energy System

## Purpose

Define the natural energy concept of VAYRANIS with clear rules and limitations.

---

# Core Principle

Natural Energy is a system to understand interactions between:

- Living organisms
- Environment
- Natural processes
- Technology development

It is not only a visual effect or symbol.

---

# Design Rules

Every energy concept must explain:

1. Source
2. Flow
3. Interaction
4. Limitations
5. Consequences

---

# Future Questions

- How do humans perceive energy?
- How do animals interact with it?
- How does technology use or measure it?
- How does it affect civilization?
