# VAYRANIS Natural Energy System

## Purpose

This section explores the relationship between life, natural forces, and the internal energy concept of the world.

---

# Design Rule

Natural Energy must have:

- Clear rules
- Limitations
- Observable effects
- Relationship with biology and environment

It should not become a decorative symbol added to every design.

---

# Prana Concept Development

Open questions:

- How does energy flow?
- How do living beings interact with it?
- Can it be measured or observed?
- What are its limitations?
