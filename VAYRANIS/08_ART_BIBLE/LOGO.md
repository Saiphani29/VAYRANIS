# Logo

This is the main logo direction for VAYRANIS.

It combines ancient river symbolism, celestial framing, gold-on-dark contrast, and a regal fantasy lettering style. Use it as the primary visual reference for the project mark, title treatment, and related identity assets.

## Reference Image

![[../Assets/VAYRANIS-Logo.png]]

## What This Shows

- A high-fantasy, archival look
- Strong use of antique gold on deep dark backgrounds
- River, serpent, star, and seal symbolism
- Formal lettering with a carved or embossed feel
- Circular emblems and ornamental framing

## Use This For

- logo refinement
- sigil exploration
- title treatment ideas
- banner or cover styling
- palette and material direction

## Notes

- This is the logo reference, not just a general mood board.
- Future art should stay compatible with its tone unless intentionally revising the brand.
