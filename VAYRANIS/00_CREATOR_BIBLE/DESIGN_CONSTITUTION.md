# Design Constitution

This document defines the rules that keep the vault coherent.

## What We Protect

- Consistency across all documents so the world does not drift.
- Clear folder boundaries so every note has a home.
- Canon notes that are easy to trace back to their source.
- A structure that works well in Obsidian and stays easy to scan.

## What We Avoid

- Duplicate canon with no clear source or explanation.
- Notes that mix too many topics and become difficult to maintain.
- Unlinked fragments that are hard to find later.
- Hard-to-scan document formatting that hides important ideas.
