# Founder Note

VAYRANIS should feel like a living reference book, not a loose set of notes.

This project is meant to support long-term worldbuilding, visual development, and story planning in a way that stays readable months or years later. That means every note should help future-you understand what was decided, why it matters, and where to look next.

## What this note is for

- It explains the spirit of the project.
- It gives the vault a human voice, not just a technical structure.
- It reminds future edits to stay organized and intentional.
