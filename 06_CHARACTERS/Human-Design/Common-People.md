# Common People Human Design

## Purpose

Common people represent the foundation of VAYRANIS society. Their designs evolve from environment, occupation, resources, and daily needs.

## Design Principles

- Practical before decorative
- Comfortable for daily life
- Connected to local materials
- Reflects region and profession

## Clothing Connection

Common clothing evolves through:

Environment → Protection → Material → Craft → Identity

## Future Development

- Regional variations
- Family traditions
- Tools and daily objects
- Community roles
