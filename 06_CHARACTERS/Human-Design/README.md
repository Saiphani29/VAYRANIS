# VAYRANIS Human Design

## Purpose

This section documents human evolution, professions, characters, and visual development.

Humans evolve through:

Environment → Survival → Skills → Society → Culture → Identity

---

## Design Principles

Human designs must show:

- Time period
- Environment
- Occupation
- Technology level
- Cultural background
- Personal story
