# Human Evolution System

## Purpose

Human design in VAYRANIS follows evolution through environment, needs, skills, society, and culture.

Humans are not designed first by appearance. They are designed by adaptation.

---

# Evolution Path

Nature
↓
Survival Needs
↓
Tools
↓
Skills
↓
Society
↓
Culture
↓
Identity

---

# Body and Environment

Early humans adapt to:

- Climate
- Terrain
- Food sources
- Travel requirements
- Protection needs

Physical design must support the world they live in.

---

# Human Categories

## Common People

Daily life shapes clothing, tools, homes, and traditions.

## Travelers

Adapted for movement, exploration, and carrying essential equipment.

## Merchants

Connected to trade routes, materials, knowledge exchange, and culture.

## Warriors

Develop specialized protection, weapons, and discipline.

## Heroes

Represent exceptional individuals developed from the same civilization systems.

---

# Design Rule

Avoid sudden modern designs.

Every human element must show:

- Why it exists
- What problem it solves
- How it evolved
- How society changed it
