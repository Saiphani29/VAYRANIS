# Hero Clothing Evolution

## Purpose

Hero clothing represents a person's journey, skills, responsibilities, and connection with the world.

## Evolution

Common clothing → Travel clothing → Specialized equipment → Personal identity

## Design Rules

- Hero clothing must show history.
- Every object should have a reason.
- Equipment evolves through experience.

## Elements

- Travel protection
- Tools and weapons integration
- Personal symbols
- Cultural connections

## Shape Language

Spiral: evolution.
Circle: purpose and continuity.
Branching: relationships.

## Future Development

- Hero stages
- Companion systems
- Animal relationships
- Prana interaction
