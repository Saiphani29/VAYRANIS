# Traveler Clothing Evolution

## Purpose

Traveler clothing evolves around movement, survival, and adaptation between regions.

## Requirements

- Protection from climate changes
- Storage for tools and supplies
- Easy movement
- Durable materials

## Evolution

Local clothing → Travel adaptation → Multi-region clothing system

## Design Elements

- Layered protection
- Carrying systems
- Repairable materials
- Weather adaptation

## Shape Language

Wave: movement and journey.
Branching: connections between places.
Circle: belonging and return.

## Future Development

- Explorer classes
- Merchant travelers
- Hero travel equipment
