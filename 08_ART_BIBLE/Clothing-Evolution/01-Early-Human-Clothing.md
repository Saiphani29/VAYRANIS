# Early Human Clothing Evolution

## Status
🟡 Foundation Draft

## Purpose

Clothing in VAYRANIS does not begin as fashion. It begins as a response to environment, survival, movement, and protection.

The evolution follows:

Nature → Need → Material → Function → Craft → Culture → Identity

---

# Stage 1: Natural Protection

Early humans first use available natural materials.

Examples:

- Animal hides
- Plant fibers
- Leaves
- Bark materials
- Natural coverings

The purpose is protection from:

- Weather
- Terrain
- Travel conditions
- Physical danger

---

# Stage 2: Basic Construction

Humans begin understanding:

- Binding
- Layering
- Wrapping
- Attaching materials

Clothing becomes more reliable and reusable.

---

# Stage 3: Regional Evolution

Different environments create different clothing systems.

Hot regions:
- Light materials
- Breathability
- Movement

Cold regions:
- Multiple layers
- Insulation
- Body coverage

Forest regions:
- Flexible materials
- Protection from plants and animals

---

# Body Coverage Evolution

The upper body covering develops because protection and comfort become important.

A shirt-like garment is not created suddenly as modern clothing. It evolves from:

Covering → Wrapping → Secured Layer → Structured Garment

---

# Lower Body Clothing

Lower garments evolve based on:

- Travel distance
- Riding
- Work requirements
- Climate
- Cultural habits

Different societies may develop different solutions.

---

# VAYRANIS Design Rule

Do not design modern clothing and place it into the past.

Every garment must answer:

1. Why was it needed?
2. What material created it?
3. What problem did it solve?
4. How did society transform it?
