# Common People Clothing Evolution

## Purpose

Common clothing represents daily survival, work, environment, and community identity.

## Evolution Path

Protection → Comfort → Work Function → Regional Style → Cultural Identity

## Design Principles

- Clothing should support daily life.
- Materials depend on local resources.
- Decoration develops after function.

## Key Elements

- Practical upper garments
- Weather protection
- Work-friendly lower garments
- Simple accessories
- Repairable construction

## Shape Language Connections

Leaf/Vine: natural materials and growth.
Wave: movement and daily flow.
Circle: community and continuity.

## Future Development

- Regional variations
- Occupation-based clothing
- Textile evolution
