# Royal Clothing Evolution

## Purpose

Royal clothing represents responsibility, history, craftsmanship, and civilization.

## Evolution

Practical clothing → Cultural clothing → Ceremonial identity

## Design Principles

- Meaning before luxury
- Craftsmanship reflects knowledge
- Natural systems inspire patterns

## Elements

- Advanced textiles
- Jewelry integration
- Shape language symbols
- Ceremony-based variations

## Shape Language

Sun: guidance.
Circle: continuity.
Diamond: craftsmanship.
Lotus and natural geometry remain cultural systems to develop separately.

## Future Development

- Crown connection
- Regional royal styles
- Ceremony clothing
