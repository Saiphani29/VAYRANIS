# Warrior Clothing Evolution

## Purpose

Warrior clothing evolves from protection, mobility, and battlefield requirements.

## Design Principles

- Protection before decoration
- Movement before weight
- Materials match technology level

## Evolution

Survival protection → Combat equipment → Cultural warrior identity

## Design Elements

- Layered protection
- Flexible construction
- Tool integration
- Symbolic markings earned through experience

## Shape Language

Mountain: strength and foundation.
Diamond: precision.
Stepped Form: advancement.

## Future Development

- Warrior groups
- Regional fighting styles
- Armor evolution
