# Clothing Evolution Development Rules

## Purpose

Clothing evolves from human needs, environment, materials, and culture.

It should never appear as a random fashion choice.

---

# Evolution Path

Environment
↓
Protection
↓
Materials
↓
Craft
↓
Social Identity
↓
Culture

---

# Early Clothing Logic

Initial clothing focuses on:

- Protection from environment
- Comfort during travel
- Safety
- Tool carrying
- Social communication

---

# Upper Garment Development

The upper body becomes important because humans need:

- Protection
- Climate adaptation
- Storage
- Identity

The shirt-like form evolves through function before style.

---

# Lower Garment Development

Lower body clothing evolves according to:

- Terrain
- Movement
- Work
- Combat
- Climate

Modern forms should not appear immediately. They must develop through needs.

---

# Clothing Groups

## Common People
Practical daily clothing.

## Travelers
Lightweight, durable, adaptable clothing.

## Merchants
Clothing showing trade, region, and material access.

## Warriors
Protection combined with mobility.

## Royal Clothing
Symbolism, craftsmanship, ceremony, and responsibility.
