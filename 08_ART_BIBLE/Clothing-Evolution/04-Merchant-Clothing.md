# Merchant Clothing Evolution

## Purpose

Merchant clothing combines travel, trade, identity, and practical business needs.

## Design Needs

- Long-distance movement
- Storage of goods and documents
- Social recognition
- Protection during trade routes

## Evolution

Traveler clothing → Trade specialization → Professional identity

## Design Elements

- Organized pockets and containers
- Durable textiles
- Regional influences
- Symbolic markings

## Shape Language

Branching: trade networks.
Ring: connections and exchange.
Wave: movement between regions.

## Future Development

- Merchant guild clothing
- Trade route variations
- Cultural exchange designs
