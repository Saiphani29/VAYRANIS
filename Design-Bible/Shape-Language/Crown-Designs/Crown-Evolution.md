# VAYRANIS Crown Evolution

## Status
🟡 Draft — Royal Design System

## Philosophy

The Bhurayan crown is not a symbol of decoration alone. It evolves from the same design principles as the civilization:

Nature → Observation → Function → Craft → Culture → Symbol

A crown represents responsibility, connection, and knowledge rather than only power.

---

# Shape Language Connections

## Circle
Continuity, unity, and connection between ruler, people, and nature.

## Diamond
Precision, craftsmanship, and refined material knowledge.

## Sun
Light, guidance, and responsibility.

## Leaf / Vine
Life, ecology, and connection with the natural world.

## Spiral
Evolution, learning, and transformation.

## Concentric Ring
Layers of history and inherited knowledge.

## Branching
Civilization networks and relationships.

## Stepped Form
Growth, ascent, and accumulated wisdom.

---

# Design Rules

- Avoid direct historical copying.
- Use evolved geometry instead of random ornament.
- Materials must have cultural and functional meaning.
- Natural motifs should represent systems, not only beauty.
